\section*{The decisive test: the local/background Hubble ratio (H0DN $\times$ BG1), the amendment and the opening}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The decisive test: the local/background Hubble ratio (H0DN $\times$ BG1), the amendment and the opening}
\textbf{The law and the question.} Null: $H_{\rm local}/H_{\rm background}=1$ ($\Lambda$CDM). Alternative: $K=E(z_\star)^{2\beta/3}$, with $\beta=\alpha\sqrt e$ at runtime and zero free parameters ($K=1.083807$, $\log_{10}K=0.034952$, recomputed in this rite). The question: does the local number of the H0DN network, read against the BG1 background, fit ratio 1 or ratio $K$?
\textbf{What v410 had pre-registered and why it did not open.} v410 pre-registered the verdict function and read no observed data; the operator's order was not to open without discriminating power, and the published vector did not fit the support of the published covariance. With the same numbers read now, the frozen v410 function (the one in \texttt{um.py}) returns branch (1): \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE\_\allowbreak{}SYSTEMATICS\_\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}RETRODICTION\_\allowbreak{}WITHOUT\_\allowbreak{}FIT\_\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}NOT\_\allowbreak{}A\_\allowbreak{}CREDITATION\_\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}GATE\_\allowbreak{}UNMOVED} --- the outcome the old rule would give, stated openly.
\textbf{The support finding and the amendment.} The published error table declares zero variance in 72 directions, touching 111 rows (37 hosts in the three anchors); the collaboration resolves it by pseudo-inverse, and the weights frozen on 6 Nov are its (the collaboration's) and give zero weight to that component. On 11 Nov the operator amended the rule (D162): branch (1) no longer refuses the vector outside the support and became gate 1 (the reproduction of the published local number and the fingerprint). The new literal differs from the old only by the amendment mark, and branches 2 to 6 are the same (checked in this rite). It is an amendment made after the guard, and the test is not blind.
\textbf{The ratified design (D159).} The primary is the published error model, with the 13 extra calibrators out (design $D=6.71$); the free-Cepheid line goes beside it, always ($D=5.19$); the gate is 90\,\% design power in the published model, with the stress lines beside.
\textbf{Publication before the opening.} The pre-registration was published in commit \texttt{769fcb2697d8} (push 2026-11-12 10:45:40 UTC); the opening began 2026-11-12 10:46:34 UTC, once; the result was published in commit \texttt{f24d954a3b90} (push 2026-11-12 10:55:51 UTC).
\textbf{The result (the two lines together).} In the primary, under the published error model, $Z$ against the null $=6.11$ (one-sided $p$ $5.1\times10^{-10}$) and $Z$ against $K=-0.61$, branch (4); beside it, in the same item, with free Cepheids, $Z$ against the null $=4.38$ and $Z$ against $K=-0.81$, branch (6), below 5. In km/s/Mpc: the background $68.53$, the law's prediction $74.27$, the reading $73.74$ and $73.35$. A retrodiction without fit; the test is not blind; proved is validity, not creditation; the gate does not move.

\textbf{What the test does not decide.} the creditation; the specificity (the Z against the null measures the Hubble tension; what belongs to the law K is the size, the Z against K); whether the published error budget is right; the independence between local and background; it is not independent evidence from Phases 4, 5 and 9.
\textbf{The seven reservations} (section 8 of the pre-registration, in Portuguese). (1) O ramo (1) foi emendado em 11/11/2026, depois de se saber que o vetor publicado o reprovava; pela função da v410 o desfecho era inconclusivo por sistemática. O teste não é cego. (2) O resultado do primário é previsível por dois agregados públicos, a menos de 0,18 desvio; o rito confere a conta, não descobre um número novo. É retrodição sem ajuste. (3) A linha das Cefeidas livres acompanha o resultado, no mesmo item. O esperado dela pelos agregados é 4,5, com 23 \% de chance de passar de 5. Nas três linhas de estresse de covariância o esperado também fica abaixo de 5. (4) O poder é de desenho no modelo publicado; as linhas de estresse vão ao lado. (5) A tabela de erros publicada declara zero em 72 direções; nelas, a maior componente do vetor publicado, na base do estimador congelado, é 0,0931 mag (o número depende da base; a norma, que não depende, sai no resultado). Isso não move o valor estimado, mas pesa no crédito do desvio. (6) O primário, o portão de 90 \% e as hipóteses foram escolhidos com a tabela de poder à vista; os agregados dos dois lados são públicos. (7) As tentativas anteriores e a sobreposição dos dados locais (seção 9).
