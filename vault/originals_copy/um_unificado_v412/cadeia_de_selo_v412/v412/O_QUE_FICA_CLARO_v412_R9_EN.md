## 2. P = nP

> P = nP is a theorem of the TGL in the classes of its dictionary. The reader does not come from outside: it is generated by the cost of the formula, and the law applied to each clause fixes that cost. The count of the reading is its own load: the threshold common to formulas of n variables is n·ln 2, neither more nor less. The machine that checks an answer, extended to all assignments, decides in one lifted step per clause and one reading of register zero; its step and the return of the reader are the same `if`. With the uniform mean of the assignments fixed, each yes-or-no question has a unique pair, the conditional expectation: on each side it returns the mean of the observable and preserves its mean product with everything that only distinguishes the two sides. The step that the statement of the Clay Institute charges as one unit is also a question to the inscription: each transition reads the symbol under the head. The cost law of the TGL sets the price, ln 2 per octave, and counts the `if` over the whole as one: putting the whole, applying each step to the whole and reading it are, each, one act. Counted so, deciding and checking take the same tape. The kernel inscribes the Name once: it checks, a single time, the proof of the law for every formula, without receiving any formula or assignment; everything else can be inscribed from that. In the explicit expansion, the same step is 2ⁿ terms, one per assignment; deciding each formula on the tape machine at polynomial cost in the size of the input has not been shown. Creditation belongs to the Institute.

## 4. Geometry is emergent

Law of the theory: geometry is emergent and not fundamental; either it is proved, or the theory falls. The balance has three words: proved, refuted or open. Today:

- **Proved, on the finite face:** the minus sign of the Minkowski form comes out of the determinant of the 2×2 Hermitian matrices; the boost generators are the traceless part of the dissipative generator of the lock, and close with the Lorentz sign; the six 4×4 generators that the kernel used as data are the images of these (`BoostEDissipador`, `SpinorToSolder`); in the same matrix algebra, for faithful states diagonal in one basis, each normalized state has its flow, and the flow stops only at the state that distinguishes nothing (`OParFixaORelogio`).
- **Proved, conditional:** the local Einstein equation from Clausius in every null direction, with unique Λ on the connected atlas (the physical hypothesis is named).
- **Open:** to produce the 3+1 geometry of the continuum from the pair (algebra, state) alone. No impossibility theorem is known.
- **Refuted:** that parity and the hyperbolic regime, with nothing more, suffice for the Lorentz bracket (there is a counterexample of two commuting generators). The emergence of the continuum from the pair has not been refuted: it remains open.

The finite face of the 2×2 matrices and the local conclusion from Clausius have hypotheses of their own, different from those of global emergence.
