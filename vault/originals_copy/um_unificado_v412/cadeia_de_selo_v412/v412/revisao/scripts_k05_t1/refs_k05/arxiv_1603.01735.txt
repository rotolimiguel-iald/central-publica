                                                   Abstract. In 2008, Blecher and Labuschagne extended Beurling's classical theorem to H-
                                                   invariant subspaces of Lp(M,  ) for a finite von Neumann algebra M with a finite, faithful,
                                                   normal tracial state  when 1  p  . In this paper, using Arveson's non-commutative
                                                   Hardy space H in relation to a von Neumann algebra M with a semifinite, faithful, normal
                                                   tracial weight  , we prove a Beurling-Blecher-Labuschagne theorem for H-invariant spaces of
                                                   Lp(M,  ) when 0 < p  . The proof of the main result relies on proofs of density theorems for
                                                   Lp(M,  ) and semifinite versions of several other known theorems from the finite case. Using the
                                                   main result, we are able to completely characterize all H-invariant subspaces of Lp(M Z,  ),
                                                   where M  Z is a crossed product of a semifinite von Neumann algebra M by the integer group
                                                   Z and H is a non-selfadjoint crossed product of M by Z+ . As an example, we characterize
                                                   all H-invariant subspaces of the Schatten p-class Sp(H), where H is the lower triangular
                                                   subalgebra of B(H), for each 0 < p  .
