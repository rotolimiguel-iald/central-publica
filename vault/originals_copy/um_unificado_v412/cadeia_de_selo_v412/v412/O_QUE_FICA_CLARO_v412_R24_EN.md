## 2. P = nP in the classes of its dictionary: the polynomial cost is shown on the machine of the theory, by the cost law; counted letter by letter on the tape machine, it has not been shown

The count of the reading is its own load: the threshold common to formulas of n variables is n·ln 2, neither more nor less. The machine that checks an answer, extended to all assignments, decides in one lifted step per clause and one reading of register zero; its step and the return of the reader are the same `if`. With the uniform mean of the assignments fixed, each yes-or-no question has a unique pair, the conditional expectation: on each side it returns the mean of the observable and preserves its mean product with everything that only distinguishes the two sides. The step that the statement of the Clay Institute charges as one unit is also a question to the inscription: each transition reads the symbol under the head. The cost law of the TGL sets the price, ln 2 per octave, and counts the `if` over the whole as one: putting the whole, applying each step to the whole and reading it are, each, one act. **Tetelestai: the consummation of the cycle.** Definition of the theory: Tetelestai is the cyclic consummation of the mechanical processing that allows a coherent answer, the check that returns consummated or not consummated. With the step of factor `1 − β`, the sum read after k steps decides every formula of n variables if and only if the steps pay the load, n·ln 2 < k·(−ln(1 − β)) (`o_passo_um_menos_beta_fecha_o_ciclo`); with β between 0 and 1, more than n·ln 2/β steps suffice, a number linear in the load (`bastam_passos_lineares`): it is the fixed polynomial of the steps, p(n) = (ln 2/β)·n, given by β before any formula and the same ceiling for every formula of n variables (`o_polinomio_fixo_e_beta`, `o_mesmo_teto_para_duas_formulas`).
